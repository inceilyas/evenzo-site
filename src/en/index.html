What it does

  • Personal channels

    Set a monthly budget, log what you spend, and see how you are doing. Personal channels are free and stay on your iPhone.

  • Group channels

    Split costs for a household or a trip with up to 10 people. Everyone sees the same balances and who owes whom. Joining a group is free.

  • No bank connection

    You enter expenses yourself, so the app never asks for bank logins. Multiple currencies are supported.

Your data

Personal channels are stored only on your phone. Group channels are stored on our servers so that everyone in the group sees the same numbers, and only the members of the group can read them. There is no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
