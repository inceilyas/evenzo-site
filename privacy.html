Evenzo (the "App") is an iPhone budget and expense tracker. It is operated by Mehmet İlyas İnce (Miselylab) ("we", "us"), who decides how and why your personal data is handled and is the controller of that data (including under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, "GDPR"). This policy explains what the App handles, why, on what legal basis, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what choices and rights you have. Our contact details are in section 14.

1. The short version

  • No bank connection. The App never asks for bank logins and does not connect to banks or card networks. You enter expenses yourself.
  • Personal channels stay on your phone. A channel that is just for you is stored only on your iPhone. It is not sent to our servers, and the App has no iCloud sync.
  • Group channels are stored on our servers. If you create or join a group, the group's data (names, expenses, balances, activity) is stored with Google Firebase in the United States, so that everyone in the group sees the same numbers. Only the members of the group can read it.
  • No sign-up. There is no email, password or phone number. For groups, the App quietly creates an anonymous sign-in in the background. You can optionally connect it to your Apple Account so you can get your groups back on a new phone. We do not ask Apple for your name or email address.
  • No ads, no tracking. The App has no advertising software, does not track you across other companies' apps and websites, and we do not sell your data. The only statistics we keep are about purchases: our purchase service, RevenueCat, records Group Plan purchases and when the purchase screen is shown, under a random ID that is not your name or account (section 6).
  • You can delete your group account in the App (Profile > Delete group account).
  • If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA). The data we handle is processed in the United States and Türkiye, not in the EEA. Sections 7, 10 and 12 give the legal bases, your GDPR rights and the transfer safeguards.

2. Personal channels (on your phone only)

A personal ("Just me") channel, with its budget, transactions, categories, repeating rules and settings, is saved in the App's database on your iPhone. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it. The App does not use iCloud syncing for it.

  • Device backups. Like most apps, the App's data on your iPhone can be included in your device backup if you have iCloud Backup or a computer backup turned on. That backup is made and kept by Apple or by you under your own settings, not by us.
  • Recently deleted. A personal transaction you delete stays on your phone in Recently deleted for 30 days, so you can bring it back. After that it is removed from the App's database.
  • Removing everything. Deleting a channel removes it from the phone. Deleting the App removes the App's data from the phone (a restored backup can bring it back).
  • Widgets. If you use the App's Home Screen or Lock Screen widgets, the App saves a small summary for them (channel names, amounts, recent expenses and, for a group, your balance) in a storage area on your iPhone that the App shares with its own widgets. It stays on the phone.
  • Reminders. Reminders for your personal channels (a repeating entry that is due, or an optional daily reminder that is off unless you turn it on) are scheduled on your iPhone by the App itself. They are not sent by our servers.
  • Other things on your phone. Preferences, your optional profile name (shown on your home screen; it stays on this phone) and a small cache of exchange rates are also stored on the phone only.

It runs in the United States (Iowa, region us-central1). A group belongs to no single person: it keeps existing even if the person who started it leaves.

4. Sign in with Apple (optional)

After you first use a group, the App may offer "Continue with Apple". It is optional and always available in Profile. If you use it, your anonymous sign-in is linked to your Apple identity so that your groups can be recovered on a new phone. The App asks Apple for no name and no email address; only Apple's identifier for your sign-in is connected to the account. Apple's own sign-in screens and privacy practices apply to what you share with Apple.

When you delete your group account and Apple is connected, the App asks you to confirm with Apple again and asks Apple to revoke the connection. If Apple cannot be reached, nothing is deleted and you can try again.

5. Notifications

Notifications are optional. The App asks for permission when you first open a group, after explaining what they are for, or when you turn on a reminder yourself. The permission prompt is shown by iOS, and you can say no; the App then works the same, only without notifications. It registers your device with our server only if you allow notifications and have a group account. We treat your choice in that prompt as your consent to receive these notifications and to the device registration described in section 3, and you can withdraw it at any time (below). When a member adds, edits, deletes or restores an expense or settlement, our server sends a short push notification to the other members it concerns. It contains the acting member's name, the expense title, the amount and the group name (for example "Ali added Dinner ($40.00) in Trip"). The wording is shown in your phone's language. Notifications travel through Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple's push notification service. A notification about a deleted expense can offer a "Restore" button; using it restores that expense in the group in your name.

For each group you can choose all activity, only changes to your balance, or muted, and you can mute a group for a limited time (section 3). Reminders for personal channels are created on your phone and never pass through our servers (section 2). You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time; your device record then stops being useful and is removed automatically (section 8). Withdrawing consent does not affect what was sent before.

6. Who receives information

We share information only with the providers needed to run the App. Google and RevenueCat process data on our behalf and on our instructions (processors). Apple and, for exchange rates, Frankfurter are independent services that receive data from your phone for their own functions. Group members are the other recipients of group data.

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud). Hosting and processing of group data, the anonymous sign-in, notifications, App Check, and the static invite page. Google acts as our processor under the Firebase data processing and security terms. The data is stored and processed in the United States (section 12). The invite page is a static web page on Firebase Hosting; it does not read or send the invite token, but, as with any web server, Google's infrastructure may process technical request data such as the IP address and the requested address (which contains the invite link) to deliver the page.
  • Apple. App Store purchases and subscriptions (Apple handles payment and gives us signed transaction records and renewal or refund notices), push notifications, Sign in with Apple if you use it, and App Attest, which Firebase App Check uses to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the App. Apple is an independent party for its own services.
  • RevenueCat (purchases). The App uses RevenueCat, Inc. (United States) to run purchases and to show us purchase statistics. When the App starts, it creates a random ID for this install. The ID is not your name, your Apple Account or your sign-in ID. RevenueCat receives it together with technical details such as the App version, the iOS version, the device type and the App Store country and, as with any internet request, the IP address. If you buy or restore a Group Plan, RevenueCat also receives Apple's purchase record (product, price, currency, dates, renewal and refund status), and our server passes Apple's renewal and refund notices on to RevenueCat. RevenueCat also records when the purchase screen is shown. RevenueCat acts as our processor under its data processing addendum, and processes the data in the United States (section 12). It does not receive your expenses, your groups, any names or your payment card.
  • Frankfurter (exchange rates). When you enter an amount in another currency, the App requests the day's exchange rate from the free Frankfurter service (api.frankfurter.dev, based on European Central Bank data). The request contains only currency codes, such as EUR and USD. No amount, name, group or account information is sent. As with any internet request, the service can see the IP address the request comes from. The rate is saved on your phone, and it is also saved with the transaction. Frankfurter is a free public service, not a processor acting on our instructions.
  • Other members of your group, as described in section 3.
  • Authorities or successors, when required by law or to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transfer, with notice where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for advertising. The App contains no advertising or crash-reporting software, and no analytics other than the purchase statistics described above.

9. Deleting your data and leaving groups

  • Leave a group. You lose access to the group. Your name, your share of past expenses and your balance stay in the group, so that the other members' balances do not change. They can still record a settlement with you.
  • Delete group account (Profile > Delete group account). You leave all your groups, and your name there is replaced by "Deleted member" (on your member record and in activity entries about you). Your notification registrations and code-attempt counter are deleted, and your anonymous sign-in is deleted from Firebase Authentication. If Apple is connected, the connection is revoked first. Our server also asks RevenueCat to delete the purchase record kept under your random ID. Expenses and settlements you entered, paid or shared stay in the group, with their titles, so the other members' balances stay correct. This cannot be undone. It does not cancel a subscription: manage that in your Apple Account settings. Your personal channels stay on your phone.
  • Delete a personal channel or transaction. In the App. It never reached our servers.
  • Last member out. When the last active member leaves or deletes their account, the group is deleted after 30 days (section 8).
  • By email. If you cannot use the App (for example you lost your phone), write to us. Because the App has no login, we may need details such as the group name and the name you used in it, and we may not be able to find your record without them.

10. Your rights and choices

You can use the in-App tools in section 9 at any time. You can also email miselylab@gmail.com (see "By email" in section 9). We answer within one month of receiving your request. If a request is complex or we get many, the law may allow us to extend that by up to two further months, and we will tell you why. Exercising your rights is free. We may ask you to confirm that a request concerns your own record, and we will not treat you worse for using your rights.

  • European Economic Area. Under the GDPR you have the right to: (1) access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy; (2) have inaccurate data corrected (in a group, members can edit expenses and group details in the App; for anything you cannot change there, email us); (3) have your data erased (use Delete group account in the App, or email us); (4) restrict processing in the cases the GDPR lists; (5) data portability: receive the data you gave us in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, which applies to data we process because of the contract or your consent (the App has no export button, so email us and we will send it); (6) object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests (section 7); and (7) withdraw consent at any time (section 7). You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority, in particular in the EU or EEA country where you live or work or where you think your rights were infringed. You can find them through the European Data Protection Board website. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first, but you do not have to contact us before you complain. The same rights apply under UK data protection law to people in the United Kingdom, where the supervisory authority is the Information Commissioner's Office.
  • United States. Depending on your state, you may have rights to know, access, correct and delete your personal information and to appeal our decision. We do not sell personal information, do not share it for targeted advertising, and do not use it for profiling with legal or similarly significant effects. You may use an authorized agent. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our email and you may contact your state attorney general.
  • Türkiye. We are based in Türkiye. Under Article 11 of Law No. 6698 you may ask whether your data is processed, request information about it, ask for correction or deletion, and apply to us at the address in section 14. You may also complain to the Personal Data Protection Board.
  • Other countries. If your local law gives you further rights over your personal data, we will honor them as far as they apply to us.
  • Permissions on your phone. You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings at any time.

11. Children

The App is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Do not use the App if you are under 13. Where the law of your country sets a higher minimum age for consenting to the processing of personal data (in the EEA, countries may set it anywhere from 13 to 16), you must be at least that age, or have the consent of a parent or guardian, to use the App. If you believe a child's data has been sent to us, contact miselylab@gmail.com and we will delete it.

12. International transfers and security

We are based in Türkiye and have no establishment in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. The App's backend processes group data in the United States (Google Firebase, Iowa), and RevenueCat, Inc. processes purchase records in the United States. We, as the controller, operate the backend and can reach group data from Türkiye. If you live in the EEA or the UK, your data is therefore transferred to the United States and handled from Türkiye, where data protection law may differ from yours. Personal channels are not part of this: they stay on your phone.

The safeguards we rely on:

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud). The Firebase data processing and security terms include the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers to countries without an adequacy decision. Google also states that Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.
  • RevenueCat. Its data processing addendum incorporates the Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK addendum to them).
  • Türkiye. There is no EU adequacy decision for Türkiye. The data we access from there stays in the Google and RevenueCat systems described above, and we apply this policy to it wherever we are.
  • Apple and Frankfurter. Apple handles data from your phone under its own terms. The Frankfurter request contains only currency codes and, as with any internet request, your IP address.

You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards that apply (for example the Standard Contractual Clauses we rely on) by emailing miselylab@gmail.com; commercial terms may be blacked out. We send only the data the App needs.

Data moves between the App and our backend over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and Google encrypts stored data at rest. Security rules let only the members of a group read and write its data, and our servers verify purchases and write the activity log. Invite links and codes are stored in our lookup records only as hashes. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.
