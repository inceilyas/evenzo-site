Support

Need help? Email us at miselylab@gmail.com. We usually reply within 3 business days. Tell us what you were doing, the name of the group if it is about a group, and add a screenshot if you can. Please do not send bank or card numbers.

Frequently asked questions

What is Evenzo?

An iPhone app for budgets and shared expenses. You organize money in channels: a personal channel for yourself, or a group channel to split costs with up to 10 people, such as a household or a trip. There is no bank connection, and you enter expenses yourself. See the Privacy Policy for where the data lives.

How do I start a group?

Tap New channel on the home screen, choose Group, then enter a group name and the main currency. You can set an end date for things like trips, and add people by name if they have not installed the App yet. Creating a group needs the Group Plan (see below). Joining a group is free.

How do I invite people? What is an invite link or code?

Open the group and tap Invite. You can share a link or show a QR code, and the same invite also has a 10-character code. People who already have the App open the link and join. People who do not have it yet install the App first, then tap the link again. If someone prefers to type, they tap New channel, choose "Have an invite code?" and enter the code.

Anyone who has the link or code can join until a member resets it. Reset it from the Invite screen (Reset link). The old link, QR code and code stop working right away. People already in the group stay in.

What are placeholders ("Not joined yet")?

A member can add a person by name before that person has the App. Their expenses can be recorded right away. When they join with the invite link, they pick their name from the list and the earlier expenses become theirs. If the wrong person is picked, any member can undo it from the Members screen. Placeholders count toward the limit of 10 people.

Can everyone edit everything?

Yes. Any member can add, edit and delete any expense. Every change is written to the Activity history, which everyone in the group can see. A deleted expense stays in Recently deleted for 30 days, and any member can restore it.

What does "Settle up" do?

It only records that people paid each other back. The App does not move money. You pay outside the App (cash, a bank app, or a payment app), then mark it as settled so the balances update.

What happens when the Group Plan ends?

The groups it funded stay fully usable for 3 more days. After that they become read-only: nobody can add, change or delete anything, but everyone can still view the group, join and leave. Any member with an active Group Plan can reopen a read-only group with their own purchase. One purchase can power up to 10 groups. Joining groups never needs a plan.

What does the Group Plan cost? Is there a free trial?

US prices are $2.99 per month, $24.99 per year and $49.99 for lifetime; your country's price is shown in the App Store. There is no free trial. Personal budgets are free. The Group Plan also unlocks the Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

How do I cancel, restore or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed in your Apple Account settings, or from Profile > Manage subscription in the App. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Profile and tap Restore Purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I keep my groups when I get a new phone?

Connect your Apple Account first: Profile > Continue with Apple. Without it, a new phone starts a new anonymous sign-in and cannot get back into your old groups on its own. Personal channels are stored only on the old phone, so use a device backup or transfer to move them.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. For groups: Profile > Delete group account. You leave all your groups, your name becomes "Deleted member", your notification registrations and sign-in are deleted, and the connection to Apple is revoked. Expenses you entered stay in the groups so other members' balances stay correct. Personal channels never leave your phone, and you delete them from the App. If you lost your phone or cannot open the App, email us with the group name and the name you used. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Where is my data?

Personal channels are only on your iPhone and are not sent to us. Group data is stored with Google Firebase in the United States, and only the members of the group can read it. We do not collect your bank details, contacts or location. Exchange rates are fetched with currency codes only. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I get notifications?

If you allow them, the App notifies you when someone adds or changes an expense that involves you. You can turn them off in iOS Settings.
