1. What the App does

The App helps you track budgets and expenses in "channels". A personal channel is for you alone. A group channel is shared with up to 10 people (including people who were added by name and have not joined yet) and calculates who owes whom.

  • Personal budgets are free. Personal channels are stored on your iPhone (see our Privacy Policy).
  • Creating a group needs the Group Plan. Joining a group is always free.
  • No bank connection. The App does not connect to your bank, and you enter your expenses yourself.

You must be at least 13 years old to use the App. If the law of your country sets a higher age for consenting to the processing of personal data, you must be at least that age, or have the consent of a parent or guardian.

The App is a tool for your own record-keeping. Nothing in it is financial, tax, investment or legal advice. Budget views such as Pace, Spent and Daily are simple calculations from your entries.

5. Shared group data

A group belongs to no single person. Please keep these points in mind before you join or create one:

  • Every member can add, edit and delete every expense and settlement, including ones other members entered. There are no admin roles in this version. Every change is written to the group's activity history, which every member can see and nobody can edit.
  • A deleted expense or settlement can be restored by any member for 30 days. After that it is permanently deleted.
  • Everything you enter in a group (your display name, expense titles, amounts, categories) can be seen by all current members. Do not put in the group anything you do not want them to see.
  • Anyone who has the invite link or code can join, until a member resets the link. Share it only with people you want in the group. Any member can reset it. Resetting does not remove people who are already in.
  • You may add other people to a group by name (as placeholders). Only add names of people who are fine with that, and use real or agreed names. When they join, they pick their name and the expenses become theirs.
  • If you leave, you lose access, but your name, your share of past expenses and your balance stay in the group. If you delete your group account, your name becomes "Deleted member" and your entries stay (see the Privacy Policy).
  • A group ends when all its members have left. It is then deleted after 30 days (unless someone joins again). A group with an end date moves to Archived after that date.
